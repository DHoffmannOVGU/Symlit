# Symlit

Symlit is a lightweight, MBSE-style modeling workspace that runs in your browser. You define
your own **metamodels** (the types and relationships you care about) and then build **models**
typed by them, on a diagram canvas.

This is the **local edition**: one Node.js process on your own machine, no accounts and no
cloud services.
